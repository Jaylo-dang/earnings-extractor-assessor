908 Devices Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Achieves Record Revenue

Revenue increased 35% compared to prior year

Raises low end of 2026 revenue outlook

​

​

BURLINGTON, Mass. – November 10, 2026 – 908 Devices Inc. (Nasdaq: MASS), a core small-cap growth company focused on purpose-built handheld chemical analysis tools for vital health, safety, and defense tech applications, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

​

"We had a strong third quarter, driven by our FTIR portfolio, with revenue growing 35% year-over-year to $19 million and device shipments reaching a new company record,” said Kevin J. Knopp, CEO and Co-founder. "Our proven platforms are winning orders across the globe as customers accelerate efforts to modernize their detection capabilities and stay ahead of an evolving threat landscape. NIRLab is also gaining traction, both in unit volumes and pipeline, as we build a growing base of connected, recurring revenue. With continued operational discipline and healthy customer funding supporting demand, we are well positioned as we head toward the end of the year.”

​

Recent Highlights

●Revenue of $19.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, increasing 35% year-over-year
●Recurring revenue of $5.1 million, representing 27% of total revenues for the quarter
●Gross margin of 53% and adjusted gross margin of 58% for the third quarter of 2026
●Net loss from continuing operations of $4.6 million and Adjusted EBITDA loss of $1.7 million for the third quarter of 2026
●Ended the quarter with a strong balance sheet, with a cash position of $99.1 million
●Shipped approximately 100 NIRLab devices in the first full quarter following the acquisition

​

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

​

Revenue was $19.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, a 35% increase over the prior year period, driven by an increase in product revenue for FTIR devices and the addition of NIRLab revenue. The installed base grew 26% year-over-year to 4,409 devices, with 308 devices placed during the third quarter, a new company record. Recurring revenue represented 27% of total revenues in the quarter.

​

Gross profit was $10.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $7.4 million for the corresponding period in the prior year. GAAP gross margin was 53% as compared to 53% for the corresponding prior year period. Adjusted gross profit was $10.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $8.1 million for the corresponding period in the prior year. Adjusted gross margin was 58%, as compared to 58% for the corresponding prior year period.

​

Operating expenses were $15.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $23.7 million for the corresponding prior year period. The $8.2 million decrease was primarily driven by an $11.0 million noncash reduction in expense related to the change in fair value of contingent consideration, partially offset by a $2.8 million increase in selling, general and administrative expenses.

​

Net loss from continuing operations was $4.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a net loss from continuing operations of $14.9 million for the corresponding prior year period. Adjusted EBITDA was a loss of $1.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a loss of $1.8 million for the corresponding period in the prior year.

​

Net loss attributable to common stockholders was $4.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $15.0 million for the corresponding prior year period.

​

Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $99.1 million as of September 30, 2026, with no debt outstanding.

​

2026 Guidance

​

908 Devices updates its full year revenue guidance range to $68.5 million to $70.0 million (from $68.0 million to $70.0 million previously), raising the low end of the range and representing 22% to 25% growth compared to 2025 revenue.

About 908 Devices

​

908 Devices is revolutionizing chemical analysis with its simple handheld devices, addressing life-altering applications. The Company’s devices are used at the point-of-need to interrogate unknown and invisible materials and provide quick, actionable answers in vital health, safety and defense tech applications, addressing the fentanyl and illicit drug crisis, toxic carcinogen exposure, and global security threats. The Company designs and manufactures innovative products that bring together the power of complementary analytical technologies, software automation, and machine learning. For more information, visit www.908devices.com.

Non-GAAP Measures of Financial Performance

​

To supplement the Company’s financial statements, which are presented on the basis of U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), the following non-GAAP measures of financial performance are included in this release and presented with detailed reconciliations to comparable GAAP financial results in the tables below:

​

●Adjusted gross profit is defined as gross profit excluding intangible amortization, acquisition and integration costs, restructuring charges (including the costs of severance), and non-cash expenses related to stock-based compensation.
●Adjusted gross margin is defined as adjusted gross profit expressed as a percentage of total revenue.
●Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) from continuing operations excluding other income, benefit for income taxes, depreciation, intangible amortization, acquisition and integration costs, restructuring charges (including the costs of severance), non-cash expenses related to stock-based compensation, and costs associated with contingent consideration related to the Company’s acquisitions and for which the conditions for payment have not yet been achieved.

​

The Company has not reconciled its guidance for adjusted gross margin and adjusted EBITDA to GAAP gross margin and GAAP net loss from continuing operations, respectively, in reliance on the exception provided by Item 10(e)(1)(i)(B) of Regulation S-K, because certain reconciling items that affect these measures, including changes in the fair value of contingent consideration related to the NIRLAB acquisition, acquisition and integration related costs, and income tax effects, have not occurred, are outside the Company’s control, or cannot be reasonably predicted. Accordingly, reconciliations to the most directly comparable GAAP measures are not available without unreasonable effort. These unavailable items could significantly affect the Company’s GAAP results.

​

The Company’s non-GAAP financial results presented in this earnings release exclude certain costs that management believes do not have a direct correlation to future business operations, nor do the resulting charges recorded accurately reflect the performance of ongoing operations for the period in which such charges are recorded, nor do the resulting charges recorded accurately reflect the anticipated cash flows of ongoing operations, and as such, excluding these costs allows management to understand and evaluate core operating performance and trends. However, as there are no standardized methods of calculating these non-GAAP financial measures, the Company’s methods may differ from those used by other companies in its industry, and accordingly, the use of these measures may not be directly comparable to similar measures used by others, thus limiting their usefulness for purposes of comparison. Furthermore, these non-GAAP measures have certain limitations since they do not include the impact of certain expenses and cash flows that are reflected in the Company’s GAAP financial results. Accordingly, when analyzing the Company’s operating performance and guidance, investors should not consider non-GAAP measures in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, comparable financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. Rather, the Company believes that these non-GAAP financial measures, when viewed in addition to and not in lieu of reported GAAP financial results, provide investors with additional meaningful information to assess financial performance and trends, enable comparison of financial results between periods, and allow for greater transparency with respect to key metrics utilized internally in analyzing and operating the Company’s business.

Dillard’s, Inc. Reports Third Quarter

and Year-to-Date Results

​

LITTLE ROCK, Ark. (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – November 12, 2026 - Dillard’s, Inc. (TXSE: DDS) (the “Company” or “Dillard’s”) announced operating results for the 13 and 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026. This release contains certain forward-looking statements. Please refer to the Company’s cautionary statements included below under “Forward-Looking Information.”

​

Dillard’s Chief Executive Officer William T. Dillard, II commented on the quarter, “We achieved 1% sales growth on top of last year’s 3% increase, supporting a strong 45.3% retail gross margin. As we head into the holidays, our stores and dedicated team are ready to welcome and serve our customers.”

​

Highlights of the Third Quarter (compared to the prior year third quarter):

​

•Total retail sales increased 1%
•Comparable store sales increased 1%
•Net income of $130.9 million compared to $129.8 million
•Earnings per share of $8.38 compared to $8.31
•Retail gross margin of 45.3% of sales compared to 45.3% of sales
•Operating expenses were $448.9 million (30.3% of sales) compared to $440.4 million (30.0% of sales)
•Cash and short-term investments of approximately $1.385 billion compared to $1.334 billion
•Ending inventory increased 4%

​

Third Quarter Results

​

Dillard’s reported net income for the 13 weeks ended October 31, 2026 of $130.9 million, or $8.38 per share, compared to $129.8 million, or $8.31 per share, for the 13 weeks ended November 1, 2025.

​

Sales – Third Quarter

​

Net sales for the 13 weeks ended October 31, 2026 and November 1, 2025 were $1.480 billion and $1.469 billion, respectively. Net sales includes the operations of the Company’s construction business, CDI Contractors, LLC (“CDI”).

​

Total retail sales (which excludes CDI) for the 13 weeks ended October 31, 2026 and November 1, 2025 were $1.418 billion and $1.401 billion, respectively. Total retail sales increased 1% for the 13 weeks ended October 31, 2026 compared to the 13 weeks ended November 1, 2025. Sales in comparable stores for the same period increased 1%.

​

Compared to the prior year third quarter as a percentage, sales increased moderately in home and furniture, men’s apparel and accessories, ladies’ accessories and lingerie, and juniors’ and children’s apparel. Sales in cosmetics increased slightly. Sales were essentially flat in shoes and decreased slightly in ladies’ apparel.

​

Gross Margin – Third Quarter

​

Consolidated gross margin for the 13 weeks ended October 31, 2026 was 43.6% of sales compared to 43.4% of sales for the 13 weeks ended November 1, 2025.

​

Retail gross margin for the 13 weeks ended October 31, 2026 was 45.3% of sales compared to 45.3% of sales for the 13 weeks ended November 1, 2025.

​

Compared to the prior year third quarter as a percentage, retail gross margin increased moderately in men’s apparel and accessories and home and furniture. Retail gross margin increased slightly in shoes and decreased slightly in ladies’ apparel, cosmetics and ladies’ accessories and lingerie. Retail gross margin declined moderately in juniors’ and children’s apparel.

​

Selling, General & Administrative Expenses – Third Quarter

​

Consolidated selling, general and administrative expenses (“operating expenses”) for the 13 weeks ended October 31, 2026 were $448.9 million (30.3% of sales) and $440.4 million (30.0% of sales) for the 13 weeks ended November 1, 2025. The increase is largely due to higher payroll expense.

​

Highlights of the 39 Weeks (compared to the prior year 39 Weeks):

​

•Total retail sales increased 2%
•Comparable store sales increased 2%
•Net income of $479.1 million compared to $366.5 million
•Earnings per share of $30.68 compared to $23.39
•Retail gross margin of 44.0% of sales compared to 42.9% of sales
•Operating expenses were $1,336.5 million (29.3% of sales) compared to $1,296.3 million (28.7% of sales)

​

39-Week Results

​

Dillard’s reported net income for the 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026 of $479.1 million, or $30.68 per share, compared to $366.5 million, or $23.39 per share, for the 39 weeks ended November 1, 2025. Included in net income for the 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026 are these items:

​

●$37.2 million ($28.4 million after tax, or $1.82 per share) in refunds of International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) tariffs
●a pre-tax gain on litigation settlement, net of legal fees, of $104.1 million ($79.6 million after tax or $5.10 per share) related to the Company’s favorable settlement of a long-standing lawsuit involving payment card interchange fees

​

Included in net income for the 39 weeks ended November 1, 2025 is a pretax gain of $5.5 million ($4.2 million after tax or $0.27 per share) primarily related to the sale of four properties.

​

Sales – 39 Weeks

​

Net sales for the 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026 and November 1, 2025 were $4.556 billion and $4.511 billion, respectively.

​

Total retail sales for the 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026 and November 1, 2025 were $4.391 billion and $4.315 billion, respectively. Total retail sales increased 2% for the 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026 compared to the 39 weeks ended November 1, 2025. Sales in comparable stores for the same period increased 2%.

​

Gross Margin – 39 Weeks

​

Consolidated gross margin for the 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026 was 42.6% of sales compared to 41.3% of sales for the 39 weeks ended November 1, 2025.

​

Retail gross margin for the 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026 was 44.0% of sales compared to 42.9% of sales for the 39 weeks ended November 1, 2025. Retail gross margin was positively impacted (80 basis points of sales) by the aforementioned $37.2 million IEEPA tariff refunds.

​

Selling, General & Administrative Expenses – 39 Weeks

​

Operating expenses for the 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026 were $1.337 billion (29.3% of sales) and $1.296 billion (28.7% of sales) for the 39 weeks ended November 1, 2025. The increase is largely due to higher payroll expenses.

​

Store Information

The Company has announced the upcoming closure of its Boynton Beach Mall Clearance Center in Boynton Beach, Florida (50,000 square feet). The store is expected to close in January 2027.

​

The Company operates 272 Dillard’s stores, including 28 clearance centers, spanning 30 states (totaling 46.1 million square feet) and an Internet store at dillards.com.

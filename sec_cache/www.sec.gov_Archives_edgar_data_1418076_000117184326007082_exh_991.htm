EXHIBIT 99.1

SLR Investment Corp. Announces Quarter Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Results

Net Investment Income of $0.33 Per Share for Q3 2026;

Declared Quarterly Distribution of $0.31 Per Share

NEW YORK, Nov. 03, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SLR Investment Corp. (NASDAQ: SLRC) (the “Company”, “SLRC”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) today reported net investment income (“NII”) of $17.8 million, or $0.33 per share, for the third quarter of 2026. On November 3, 2026, the Board declared a quarterly distribution of $0.31 per share payable on December 28, 2026, to holders of record as of December 14, 2026.

As of September 30, 2026, net asset value (“NAV”) was $17.83 per share, compared to $18.00 per share at June 30, 2026.

“We believe our diversified specialty finance portfolio is well suited for the current ‘higher-for-longer’ interest rate environment. It’s comprised of first lien loans predominantly secured by liquid working capital assets governed by borrowing bases that we actively manage,” said Michael Gross, Co-CEO of SLR Investment Corp. “Ninety-five percent of our record $575 million originations were in specialty finance, and we expect that to continue. Importantly, our net investment income of $0.33 per share was flat sequentially and covered our $0.31 quarterly distribution. With over $850 million of capital available to deploy, we are well positioned to support borrowers and sponsors that need liquidity.”

“At September 30th, our watchlist accounts for only 2% of the fair value of our portfolio, below our long-term average of 2.2%. We continue to have high conviction in the stability and credit quality of our portfolio,” said Bruce Spohler, Co-CEO of SLR Investment Corp. “Our software exposure remains under 1% of fair value, and only 2% of our gross income is derived from PIK interest resulting from amendments. Aside from our two investments on non-accrual, our portfolio companies continue to exhibit healthy fundamentals and perform at or above expectations.”

SLR Senior Lending Program LLC (SSLP)

As of September 30, 2026, the Company and its 50% partner, Sunstone Senior Credit L.P., had contributed combined equity capital of $95.8 million of a total $100 million equity commitment to the SSLP. At quarter end, SSLP had total commitments of $188.8 million at par and total funded portfolio investments of $177.9 million at fair value, to 25 different portfolio companies. SLR Investment Corp.’s Results of Operations Year Over Year   

Investment Income

For the fiscal quarters ended September 30, 2026, and 2025, gross investment income totaled $49.3 million and $57.0 million, respectively. The year-over-year decrease in gross investment income was primarily due to a decrease in the average size of the income producing investment portfolio.

Expenses

SLRC’s net expenses totaled $31.5 million and $35.4 million, respectively, for the fiscal quarters ended September 30, 2026, and 2025. The decrease in expenses for the year-over-year three-month periods was primarily due to lower incentive fees on lower net investment income.

Net Investment Income

SLRC’s net investment income totaled $17.8 million and $21.6 million, or $0.33 and $0.40, per average share, respectively, for the fiscal quarters ended September 30, 2026, and 2025.

Net Realized and Unrealized Gain (Loss)

Net realized and unrealized gain (loss) for the fiscal quarters ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 totaled $(10.2) million and $1.7 million, respectively.

Net Increase in Net Assets Resulting from Operations

For the fiscal quarters ended September 30, 2026, and 2025, the Company had a net increase in net assets resulting from operations of $7.6 million and $23.3 million, respectively. For the same periods, earnings per average share were $0.14 and $0.43, respectively.

Capital and Liquidity

Credit Facilities

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $627.9 million drawn on $995 million of total commitments available on its revolving credit facilities and $143.3 million of term loans outstanding.

Unsecured Debt

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $484 million of unsecured notes outstanding. The Company has $75 million, $135 million, and $50 million of unsecured notes maturing in December 2026, January 2027, and March 2027, respectively.  

Leverage

As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s net debt-to-equity ratio was 1.10x compared to 1.14x at December 31, 2025 and 1.16x at June 30, 2026. The Company’s target range is 0.9x to 1.25x net debt-to-equity.

Available Capital

As of September 30, 2026, including anticipated available borrowing capacity at the SSLP and our specialty finance portfolio companies, subject to borrowing base limits, SLRC, SSLP and our specialty finance portfolio companies had over $850 million of available capital in the aggregate.

Unfunded Commitments

As of September 30, 2026, excluding commitments of $97.7 million to SLR-CS, SLR-BC, SLR-HC ABL, SLR-EF, and SSLP, over which the Company has discretion to fund, the Company had unfunded commitments of approximately $264.0 million.

Subsequent Events

About SLR Investment Corp.

SLR Investment Corp. is a closed-end investment company that has elected to be regulated as a business development company under the Investment Company Act of 1940. A specialty finance company with expertise in several niche markets, the Company primarily invests in leveraged, U.S. upper middle market companies in the form of cash flow, asset-based, and life sciences senior secured loans.

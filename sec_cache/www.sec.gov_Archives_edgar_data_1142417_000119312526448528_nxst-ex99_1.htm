NEXSTAR MEDIA GROUP REPORTS RECORD QUARTERLY NET REVENUE OF $2.1 BILLION

Q3 Net Revenue Drives Net Income of $250 Million, Adjusted EBITDA of $756 Million, Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities of $680 Million and Adjusted Free Cash Flow of $443 Million

Delivered Record Q3 Political Advertising Revenue of $317 Million

Repaid $1.3 Billion of Debt Year-to-Date through Today and Returned $58 Million to Shareholders in Dividends in Q3

 

 

 

 

STATEMENT FROM PERRY A. SOOK, FOUNDER, CHAIRMAN AND CEO

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

“Nexstar delivered the highest quarterly net revenue in the Company’s history, reflecting the acquisition of TEGNA, Inc. and strong distribution and advertising revenue performance, including all-time high third-quarter political advertising revenue. The CW set a network record with its most-watched sports broadcast ever, as August’s New Mexico State – Florida State football game outperformed three of the major broadcast networks in primetime among Adults 25–54. NewsNation continues its momentum, growing total day viewership 9% year-over-year for the third quarter. Our strong operating performance for the first nine months of the year generated $1.1 billion in Adjusted Free Cash Flow, enabling us to reduce debt by $786 million and return $171 million to shareholders through dividends, while also funding ongoing investments in local news and sports programming. Looking ahead, we will continue to execute our playbook to drive long-term shareholder value while enhancing the programming, products and services that make Nexstar a leading choice for audiences and advertisers and an essential partner to networks and distributors.”

Financial Results

•
Net Revenue. Record quarterly net revenue of $2.13 billion, increased $935 million year-over-year, or 78.0%, primarily due to $765 million of incremental revenue from our acquisition of TEGNA and higher advertising and distribution revenue from our legacy business units.
•
Distribution Revenue. Third quarter distribution revenue of $1,091 million, increased $382 million, or 53.9%, versus the comparable prior year quarter, primarily reflecting $352 million of incremental revenue from the acquisition of TEGNA and higher revenue from our legacy business units due to increased rates, the impact of a one-time charge in the third quarter of 2025 which did not recur in the 2026 period, growth in vMVPD and other streaming subscribers, and the addition of CW affiliations on certain of our stations, partially offset by MVPD subscriber attrition and affiliation replacements on six stations previously affiliated with CBS.
•
Advertising Revenue. Third quarter advertising revenue of $1,023 million, increased $547 million, or 114.9%, from the comparable prior year quarter, primarily reflecting $405 million of incremental revenue from the acquisition of TEGNA and a $163 million increase in political advertising at our legacy business units, partially offset by lower non-political advertising due, in part, to crowd-out from political advertising. In total, Q3 political advertising revenue was $317 million, as 2026 is an election year.
•
Net Income. Third quarter net income of $250 million increased $185 million, or 284.6%, compared to the prior year quarter, primarily due to increased operating income from the acquisition of TEGNA and increased political advertising revenue generated at our legacy business units, partially offset by increased interest, income tax and one-time expenses, all in connection with the TEGNA transaction. Net income margin increased to 11.7% from 5.4% in the comparable prior year period.
•
Adjusted EBITDA. Third quarter Adjusted EBITDA of $756 million, increased $398 million, or 111.2%, compared to the prior year quarter reflecting $243 million of incremental Adjusted EBITDA from the acquisition of TEGNA and increased revenue at our legacy business units. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 35.4% compared to 29.9% in the comparable prior year period.
•
Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities. Third quarter Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities of $680 million, increased $563 million, or 481.2%, compared to the comparable prior year quarter, due primarily to an increase in net income and the impact of changes in operating assets and liabilities reflecting the timing of receipts and payments.
•
Adjusted Free Cash Flow. Third quarter Adjusted Free Cash Flow of $443 million, increased $277 million, or 166.9%, compared to the prior year quarter, due primarily to increased Adjusted EBITDA, partially offset by an increase in interest expense, capital expenditures and income tax payments, all primarily related to the acquisition of TEGNA.

Definitions and Disclosures Regarding Non-GAAP Financial Information

Adjusted EBITDA is calculated as net income, plus or (minus): transaction, other one-time and restructuring expenses, stock-based compensation expense, depreciation and amortization of intangible assets (excluding amortization of broadcast rights), amortization of basis difference of equity method investments, (gain) loss on asset disposal, impairment charges, interest expense, net, loss on extinguishment of debt, pension and other postretirement plans costs (credit), income tax expense (benefit) and other operating and non-operating expense (income). We consider Adjusted EBITDA to be an indicator of our assets’ operating performance.

Free Cash Flow is calculated as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditures.

 

Adjusted Free Cash Flow is calculated as Free Cash Flow plus or (minus): transaction, other one-time and restructuring expenses, changes in operating assets and liabilities, net of acquisitions (excluding changes in income tax payable), taxes paid on sale of assets, pension and other postretirement plans costs (credit) and other items. We consider Adjusted Free Cash Flow to be an indicator of our liquidity. We consider Adjusted Free Cash Flow to be a liquidity measure that provides useful information to management and investors about the amount of cash generated by the business that can be available for use in ongoing operations, debt payments, pension contributions, dividends, share repurchases, acquisitions and other items. Adjusted Free Cash Flow is not intended to represent the amount of cash flow available for discretionary expenditures as certain items and non-discretionary expenditures, such as changes in working capital, mandatory debt service requirements and pension contributions, are not deducted from this measure.

 

For a reconciliation of these non-GAAP financial measurements to the GAAP financial results cited in this news announcement, please see the supplemental tables at the end of this release.

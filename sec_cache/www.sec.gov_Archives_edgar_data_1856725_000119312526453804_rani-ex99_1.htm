Rani Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results; Provides Corporate Update

 

~ Accepted into the U.S. FDA’s Emerging Technology Program (ETP), enabling early FDA engagement to support development of the RaniPill® platform technology ~

 

~ Enrollment continues in RT-114 Phase 1a expansion cohort; data expected by end of 2026 ~

 

~ Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of $45.3 million as of September 30, 2026; expect to fund operations at least through the end of 2027 ~

 

 

FREMONT, Calif., November 9, 2026 -- Rani Therapeutics Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: RANI), a clinical-stage biotherapeutics company focused on oral delivery of biologics, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

 

“The third quarter marked continued progress for the RaniPill® platform and, most recently, our acceptance into the FDA’s Emerging Technology Program in October provides an opportunity to engage with the Agency on technical, manufacturing and regulatory considerations related to the RaniPill® platform,” said Talat Imran, Chief Executive Officer of Rani Therapeutics. “We expect this engagement to be valuable as we continue to invest in manufacturing automation to support further clinical development.”

 

“Combined with our rare disease, immunology, and obesity focused partnerships with Chugai, ProGen and PegBio, this milestone reflects our continued confidence in the RaniPill platform’s potential as an alternative to injectable biologics,” continued Imran. “With cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of $45.3 million, we believe we are well positioned to fund operations at least through the end of 2027 and to execute on our priorities, including additional RT-114 data by year-end.”

 

Recent Highlights:

 

•
Announced acceptance into the FDA’s Emerging Technology Program (ETP). In October 2026, Rani announced that the RaniPill® Capsule™ platform technology was accepted into the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Emerging Technology Program (ETP), which is administered within the Agency’s Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) and is designed to support the development and implementation of innovative approaches to pharmaceutical product design and manufacturing. Technologies accepted into the program must demonstrate the potential to improve pharmaceutical product quality and incorporate novel elements for which the FDA has limited review or inspection experience. Through participation in ETP, Rani expects to engage with the FDA’s Emerging Technology Team to

 

 

address technical and regulatory considerations related to the RaniPill platform, including through pre-submission meetings and potential site visits. Participation in ETP does not constitute FDA approval of the platform or any product candidate and does not alter applicable regulatory approval requirements.

 

Rani expects this engagement to be especially valuable as it works to automate its vertically integrated manufacturing process in anticipation of broader clinical use.

 

•
Participant enrollment continues in the Phase 1a study expansion cohort of RT-114. RT-114 is an orally administered RaniPill Capsule™ delivering PG-102, a GLP-1/GLP-2 dual agonist being developed by ProGen Co., Ltd. The expanded study is expected to enroll fifteen additional healthy volunteers to further characterize the oral-to-subcutaneous pharmacokinetic relationship for the RaniPill® HC by the end of 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results:

 

•
Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026 totaled $45.3 million, compared to $53.4 million as of June 30, 2026. Based on its current operating plan, Rani expects its cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities to be sufficient to fund its operations at least through the end of 2027.

 

•
Contract revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $1.7 million, compared to no contract revenue for the same period in 2025. The increase of $1.7 million was primarily attributable to the collaboration and license agreement with Chugai.

 

•
Research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $6.0 million, compared to $3.2 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $2.8 million was primarily attributable to higher compensation costs of $2.3 million, $0.3 million increase in third-party services and $0.2 million increase in facilities, materials and supplies.

 

•
General and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $4.9 million, compared to $4.0 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $0.9 million was primarily attributable to an increase of $0.7 million in third-party services and higher other expenses of $0.2 million.

 

•
Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $8.8 million, compared to $7.9 million for the same period in 2025, including stock-based compensation expense of $2.2 million and $2.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, respectively.

 

About Rani Therapeutics

Rani Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotherapeutics company focused on advancing technologies to enable the development of orally administered biologics and drugs. Rani has developed the RaniPill® Capsule™, a novel, proprietary and patented platform technology, intended to replace

 

 

subcutaneous injection or intravenous infusion of biologics and drugs with oral dosing. Rani has successfully conducted several preclinical and clinical studies to evaluate safety, tolerability and bioavailability using RaniPill® Capsule technology.

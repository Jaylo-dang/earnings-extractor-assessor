Enhanced Group Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Definitive agreement signed to acquire ~77% of DEAG Deutsche Entertainment AG as announced on November 5, 2026

Stake in DEAG to be acquired via an all-stock transaction at 5.3x FY2027E Adjusted EBITDA(1)(3), with combined 2027E revenue expected at approximately $670 million and combined 2027E Adjusted EBITDA(1)(3) expected at approximately $26 million
Concurrent $15 million straight equity PIPE announced

Third quarter revenue of $3.3 million, including increasing Live Enhanced telehealth revenues
Third quarter Net Loss of $13.5 million
Third quarter Adjusted EBITDA(1)(2) loss of $8.8 million
Cash and cash equivalents of $12.1 million as of September 30, 2026, prior to the $15 million PIPE announced on November 5, 2026, and not including anticipated $20 million in sponsorship

New York, NY, November 12, 2026 – Enhanced Group Inc. (NYSE: ENHA) (“Enhanced” or the “Company”), a sports, live entertainment and performance products company, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The results reported today reflect the stand-alone business of Enhanced Group -Enhanced Games and Live Enhanced. On November 5, 2026, the Company announced definitive agreements to acquire an approximately 77% controlling interest in DEAG Deutsche Entertainment AG (“DEAG”), a leading Pan-European Live Entertainment & Ticketing Group. Following the closing, (expected for the first quarter of 2027), Enhanced will fully consolidate DEAG. The consolidation is expected to completely change the financial profile of Enhanced Group, with approximately $670 million revenue and $26 million EBITDA expected for the full year 2027 for the combined group.
Business & Operational Highlights
•Executed exchange share agreements with a small group of DEAG shareholders to acquire a 77% controlling interest in DEAG.
•Concurrent with the DEAG exchange share agreements, executed a securities purchase agreement for a private placement to raise an additional $15 million from long-term, blue chip investors.
•Successfully completed the “Enhanced Breakers 1” event, featuring Beatriz Piron who, after having to withdraw from the inaugural Enhanced Games in Las Vegas due to an injury, returned just six weeks later following a highly personalized recovery and enhancement protocol to set a new world record in the snatch -showcasing the power of personalized enhancements.
•“Enhanced Breakers 2” will take place on December 19, 2026 at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, featuring eight swimmers, four men and four women, in a 25-meter short-course format. Enhanced Breakers events operate at a fraction of the cost of a flagship Enhanced Games event, yet provide massive visibility and fan engagement, particularly via social media.
•Launched the beta version of “Enhanced OS” today. “Enhanced OS” is an AI-powered platform designed to combine medical records, biomarkers testing and wearable data into individualized health plans, with licensed physicians retaining clinical decision-making authority.
•Expanded its offerings to include Modafinil and six additional peptides, including BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, MOTS-c, Epitalon and Semax, through its Live Enhanced telehealth platform, supplementing its existing offerings of 12 Rx products. All peptides are available by prescription.
•Amended the Rezolve sponsorship agreement on August 11, 2026 so that the commitment is a fixed dollar consideration of $10.0 million, settled in Rezolve ordinary shares, which are subject to a lock-up through December 23, 2026.
•Completed the third and final closing of its approximately $50 million private placement on August 14, 2026.

Maximilian Martin, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Enhanced, commented, “The third quarter was a deliberate breather between the inaugural Enhanced Games and the next growth acceleration. We used Q3 to refine our telehealth offering, bring Enhanced OS to beta, and entered into agreements to acquire a controlling interest in DEAG. With the acquisition of a majority stake in DEAG we are entering hyper-growth mode — not only by continuing to build the Enhanced Games franchise and the Live Enhanced telehealth business. Our goal going forward is to build, acquire, and invest in high-potential businesses globally spanning sports and live entertainment, as well as scalable commercialization platforms such as ticketing, direct-to-consumer brands, and beyond. We believe we are benefiting from powerful macro tailwinds: AI and robotics will increasingly reduce the need for human labor, giving humanity significantly more free time. We believe much of this additional leisure time will be spent on real-life, shared experiences — creating substantial opportunities in sports, entertainment, and live experiences. And we believe we are fully financed for growth.”
Sid Banthiya, Chief Financial Officer of Enhanced, stated, “Our third quarter demonstrates the operating discipline we committed to following the inaugural Enhanced Games. We have deliberately held back on aggressively scaling our telehealth platform so far, as we wanted to get the product mix right, optimize the unit economics and build something that offers more than just an online pharmacy. We have made significant progress, with important KPIs like average order values continuing to stay ahead of others in the telehealth space, and tripled marketing efficiencies.
With Enhanced OS — its beta going live today — we have developed an AI-powered platform designed to integrate medical records, offer biomarker testing, and wearable data integration into personalized health plans, with licensed physicians retaining full clinical decision-making authority.
All of that taken together, we believe we are now ready to shift into growth mode with our telehealth business towards the end of the year. Consequently, we expect telehealth revenues in 2027 to reach well into the double-digit millions.”

DEAG Transaction
As previously announced on November 5, 2026, Enhanced entered into definitive exchange agreements with certain shareholders of DEAG, a Berlin-headquartered Pan-European Live Entertainment and Ticketing company, to acquire a 77% controlling interest. DEAG operates in seven core European markets, stages approximately 6,000 live events a year across festivals, touring, local promotion and spoken word, and runs proprietary ticketing platforms. DEAG has completed more than 30 acquisitions since 2019. DEAG will continue to operate under its existing management team, led by incoming Chief Executive Officer Markus Breitenecker, and will continue its buy-and-build strategy, which Enhanced intends to support with additional growth capital.
Following the closing of the transaction, the combined group is expected to generate approximately $670 million in combined revenue and $26 million in Adjusted EBITDA(1)(3) in 2027, representing 21.5% revenue growth and an approximately $65.5 million improvement in Adjusted EBITDA year over year, providing Enhanced with significant operating scale and a broader platform for organic growth and further acquisitions. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below.
The transaction is all-stock, with no cash consideration. Enhanced expects to issue approximately 11 million shares of Class A common stock at $8.00 per share, an approximately 13% premium to the November 4, 2026 closing price of $7.11, at an exchange ratio of approximately one Enhanced share for 1.7 DEAG shares. The agreed value is EUR 4.00 per DEAG share, which values 100% of DEAG’s equity at approximately $115 million and implies an enterprise value of approximately $139 million(2). That equals 5.3x DEAG’s FY2027E Adjusted EBITDA(1)(3) of approximately $26.5 million, compared with a median of 14.1x for listed live entertainment peers. FY2027E figures are Enhanced management estimates. The newly issued shares are subject to a lock-up of up to 12 months with staggered releases.

PIPE Financing
In connection with the DEAG acquisition, Enhanced entered into a securities purchase agreement with institutional and accredited investors for a private investment in public equity (“PIPE”) financing of $15 million with selected long-term investors.
The PIPE is intended to provide additional capital to support Enhanced's growth strategy, including the further acceleration of its existing sporting business, telehealth and performance products businesses, as well as supporting DEAG’s buy-and-build strategy and general corporate purposes. It is expected to close concurrently with the DEAG acquisition, subject to customary closing conditions.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The Company refers to certain financial measures that are not recognized under United States generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). This press release includes financial measures that are not calculated in accordance with GAAP, including Adjusted EBITDA. Please see the definition below and the reconciliation table included in this release
4

for additional information and a reconciliation of the non-GAAP financial measure to the most comparable GAAP financial measure.
The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net loss excluding income taxes, net interest expense, depreciation and amortization, equity-based compensation, transaction costs, certain legal costs, restructuring and severance charges, and certain other items when applicable.
Enhanced management believes Adjusted EBITDA is useful to investors because it eliminates the significant level of non-cash and non-recurring items, including stock-based compensation and the one-time transaction cost associated with the Business Combination that affect period-over-period comparability.
Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider it in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of the Company’s results as reported under GAAP. Some of these limitations are:
• It does not reflect every cash expenditure, future requirements for capital expenditures, or contractual commitments;
• It does not reflect interest expense or the cash requirements necessary to service interest or principal payments on the Company’s obligations;
• Although depreciation and amortization are non-cash charges, the assets being depreciated and amortized will often have to be replaced or require improvements in the future, and Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect any cash requirement for such replacements or improvements;
• Equity-based compensation is a recurring component of the Company’s compensation programs, and excluding it does not reflect the full cost of retaining personnel; and
• It is not adjusted for all non-cash income or expense items reflected in the Company’s statements of cash flows.
The Company defines Live Enhanced gross revenue as the total amount billed to customers for Live Enhanced products, before deducting the amounts retained by the third-party telehealth providers that deliver the prescription services.
The Company defines performance marketing spend as cash spend on digital advertising platforms to acquire Live Enhanced customers. It excludes non-cash advertising credits received as sponsorship consideration and agency, creative and consulting costs.
These Non-GAAP measures should not be considered a substitute for the reported results prepared in accordance with GAAP and should not be considered in isolation or as an alternative to net loss as an indicator of the Company’s financial performance, as a measure of discretionary cash available to invest in the growth of its business, or as a measure of cash that will be available to meet its obligations. This non-GAAP financial measure, as determined and presented by the Company, may not be comparable to related or similarly titled measures reported by other companies.

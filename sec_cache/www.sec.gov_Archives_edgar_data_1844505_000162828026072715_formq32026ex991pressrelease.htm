QT Imaging Reports 75% Third Quarter Revenue Growth and Major Medicare Reimbursement Milestone
Revenue reaches $7.3 million in Q3 and $21.3 million year-to-date, with 43 scanners sold
CMS establishes Medicare hospital outpatient payment for diagnostic QTscan examinations, effective January 1, 2027
2026 revenue guidance adjusted to approximately $34 million due to shipment delays caused by the ongoing war in the Middle East; U.S. shipments remain on plan.
Conference call today at 4:30 p.m. Eastern
PETALUMA, Calif., November 10, 2026 – QT Imaging Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: QTI) (“QT Imaging” or the “Company”), a medical device company dedicated to transforming breast health management through innovative, radiation-free imaging technology, today reported financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.
“Our third-quarter results demonstrate the continued growth of QT Imaging, with revenue increasing 75% year over year and reaching $21.3 million for the first nine months of 2026, approximately double the prior-year period,” said Dr. Raluca Dinu, Chief Executive Officer of QT Imaging. “We are moving beyond building our commercial foundation to accelerating market adoption. A major milestone is the recent CMS decision establishing separate Medicare hospital outpatient payment for medically necessary diagnostic QTscan examinations, effective January 1, 2027. Together with our dedicated billing code, effective October 1, this decision removes a significant reimbursement uncertainty and strengthens our ability to expand into U.S. hospitals and health systems.
We have also doubled our manufacturing capacity with the relocation to our new 22,000-square-foot facility in Petaluma, California, while improving operational efficiency. Internationally, we secured our first major hospital order in the Gulf Cooperation Council region from American Hospital Dubai. With our differentiated Breast Acoustic CT™ technology, expanding commercial capabilities, and growing clinical partnerships, we are positioning QT Imaging for sustained growth and broader adoption of our technology.”
Third Quarter and Recent Business Highlights
Our progress includes:
•Strong commercial growth: Third-quarter revenue increased 75% year over year to $7.3 million, with 15 Breast Acoustic CT scanners sold. For the first nine months of 2026,


revenue reached $21.3 million, with 43 scanners sold, compared with 23 in the prior-year period.
•Expanded U.S. commercialization: Strengthened our direct sales organization, expanded our pipeline of qualified opportunities, and began receiving direct customer purchase orders, while continuing our partnership with NXC Imaging.
•Broader access to U.S. healthcare systems: Recognition at Premier's Supplier Innovation Celebration and a national group purchasing agreement secured by NXC Imaging, providing a streamlined procurement pathway to Premier's network of nearly 4,400 hospitals and health systems.
•Major Medicare reimbursement milestone: On November 4, 2026, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) reversed its earlier proposed nonpayment decision and established separate Medicare hospital outpatient payment for medically necessary diagnostic QTscan examinations, effective January 1, 2027. CMS assigned CPT Category III code 1079T to Ambulatory Payment Classification (APC) 5522, with payment status indicator S. Together with the October 1 implementation of the dedicated CPT code, this decision establishes a Medicare hospital outpatient payment pathway for eligible diagnostic QTscan examinations. The Company has developed a comprehensive coding and billing guide to support healthcare providers. This Medicare payment pathway applies to eligible, medically necessary diagnostic QTscan examinations and does not extend to routine breast cancer screening.
•Clinical adoption and evidence generation: Expanded radiologist training and continued clinical research aligned with our payer strategy, focusing on supplemental imaging for women with dense breasts, quantitative monitoring of treatment response, and diagnostic applications for high-risk women.
•International expansion: Secured our first major United Arab Emirates (“UAE”) hospital order from American Hospital Dubai through our local distributor, Al Naghi Medical, received regulatory authorization in Israel and Bahrain, and advanced plans for clinical collaborations in Israel and the UAE.
•Operational and quality milestones: Completed our relocation to the approximately 22,000-square-foot Petaluma headquarters and manufacturing facility, which doubled our manufacturing capacity, and successfully completed our first routine FDA inspection with zero Form FDA 483 observations.
•Technology and platform development: Advanced our integrated Breast Acoustic CT platform through improvements in image reconstruction, quantitative analysis, the QTI-Olea Viewer, and QTI Precision Pathway™ cloud capabilities.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $7.3 million, up 75% from $4.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to sales of 15 Breast Acoustic CT


scanners, compared with nine in the prior-year quarter, in accordance with minimum order quantities under the Company’s amended NXC Imaging distribution agreement.
Gross profit was $3.0 million, compared with $1.8 million in the prior-year quarter. Gross margin was 41.3%, compared with 43.0% a year ago, and was broadly consistent with the second quarter of 2026.
Total operating expenses were $5.5 million, compared with $3.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase primarily reflected higher research and development costs, including professional services and clinical study expenses, and higher selling, general and administrative expenses, mainly compensation and benefits associated with the Company’s expanded organization.
Operating loss was $2.4 million, compared with an operating loss of $1.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net loss was $0.3 million, or $0.02 per share, a significant improvement, compared with a net loss of $4.6 million, or $0.47 per share, for the prior-year quarter. The third-quarter 2026 net loss included a noncash gain of $2.4 million from the change in fair value of the Company’s earnout liability.
As of September 30, 2026, cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash totaled $8.4 million, compared with $11.0 million as of June 30, 2026. Accounts receivable were $7.3 million, which was collected in full by end of October, and inventory was $11.5 million as of September 30, 2026.
2026 Revenue Outlook
The Company is revising its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to approximately $34 million, from its prior guidance of approximately $39 million. The revised outlook reflects the inability to complete anticipated scanner shipments to distribution partners in Saudi Arabia and the UAE because of ongoing armed conflict and disruptions to shipping routes, including the effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz to commercial traffic.
The Company’s U.S. scanner shipment plan remains unchanged. QT Imaging continues to work with its regional distribution partners and intends to pursue shipments when conditions permit.

Because not all companies use identical calculations, our presentation of non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies.

Investors are cautioned that there are material limitations associated with the use of non-GAAP financial measures as an analytical tool. In addition, these measures may be different from non-GAAP financial measures used by other companies, even where similarly titled, limiting their usefulness for comparison purposes and therefore should not be used to compare QT Imaging’s performance to that of other companies. We endeavor to compensate for the limitation of the non-GAAP financial measures presented by also providing the most directly comparable GAAP measures and descriptions of the reconciling items and adjustments to derive the non-GAAP financial measures.

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as EBITDA further adjusted for other expense (income), change in fair value of the warrant, derivative, and earnout liabilities, loss on issuance of debt, loss on debt extinguishment and modification, and stock-based compensation. Management does not consider these non-GAAP measures in isolation or as an alternative to financial measures determined in accordance with GAAP. About QT Imaging
QT Imaging Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: QTI) is a medical device company engaged in the research, development, and commercialization of innovative imaging systems that use low-frequency sound waves. QT Imaging Holdings, Inc. strives to improve global health outcomes. Its strategy is predicated upon the fact that medical imaging is critical to the detection, diagnosis, and treatment of disease and that it should be safe, affordable, accessible, and centered on the patient’s experience.

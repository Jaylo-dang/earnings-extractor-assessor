Forum Markets Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Updates 2026 Guidance and Maintains 2027 Revenue Outlook

 

PALM BEACH, Fla., Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — Forum Markets, Incorporated (Nasdaq: FRMM) (“Forum” or the “Company”), an innovator in sourcing and operating cash-generating assets in AI infrastructure, commercial aerospace, and AI-powered consumer finance, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended Sept. 30, 2026.

 

For the third quarter of 2026, the Company reported revenue of $2.0 million, compared with $4.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. All third quarter revenue was generated by the Company’s commercial aircraft engine leases. Net income was $7.9 million, or $0.52 per basic share and $0.47 per diluted share, compared with a net loss of $216.7 million, or $174.54 per diluted share, in the prior-year period. Adjusted EBITDA was $12.8 million, compared with an Adjusted EBITDA loss of $0.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net income and Adjusted EBITDA for the current quarter included a non-cash gain of $13.9 million from the change in fair value of the Company’s digital assets.

 

“Over the past three months, we established our AI infrastructure platform through our Forum Edge AI joint venture and made it the primary focus of our capital and our growth,” said McAndrew Rudisill, chairman and chief executive officer of Forum Markets. “We have established the foundation for what we believe can become a substantial AI infrastructure business.”

 

Third Quarter and Recent Business Highlights

 

In October, Forum formed its Forum Edge AI joint venture with Edge Node to deploy high-performance GPU compute capacity across existing powered data centers and communications tower sites, an approach designed to bring capacity online faster and with less upfront capital than building new data centers.

 

 

 

 

Forum is in active discussions with multiple potential partners for its compute capacity.

 

During the third quarter of 2026, Forum deployed approximately $23.7 million to acquire two additional commercial aircraft engines, bringing its portfolio to five engines on long-term lease to two of the largest U.S. airlines.

 

“The midterm elections significantly disrupted an already tightening AI compute market, accelerating rising costs for equipment, compute and financing,” Rudisill said. “These conditions increase the value of data center capacity that is already permitted, powered and built, which is exactly what Forum has. Demand for our capacity is exceptionally strong, and we are in active discussions with multiple potential partners who want to secure it. The question is not whether we can contract our capacity. Our focus is to ensure we are contracting it on terms that make the most sense for Forum and its shareholders and to ensure we are building this business with a steady hand so that it is durable, scalable and profitable over the long term.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Selling, general and administrative expenses were $8.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $224.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily reflecting a reduction in non-cash stock-based compensation expense to $2.7 million from $208.2 million in the same prior-year period.

 

For the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue was $6.2 million, compared with $4.1 million in the same prior-year period. Net loss from continuing operations was $82.1 million, compared with a loss of $212.0 million in the prior-year period, and net loss was $81.2 million, compared with a loss of $220.8 million in the prior-year period. Adjusted EBITDA for the current nine-month period was a loss of $70.6 million, compared with a loss of $2.8 million in the prior-year period.

Balance Sheet

 

As of Sept. 30, 2026, total assets were $166.8 million, compared with $306.3 million as of Dec. 31, 2025. Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities totaled $15.4 million, compared with $12.4 million at year-end 2025. Digital assets totaled $35.5 million, compared with $61.6 million at year-end 2025, and the Company’s strategic equity investments were unchanged at $45.7 million. The Company’s staking receivables balance, which totaled $181.0 million at Dec. 31, 2025, was fully eliminated during the current period. Property and equipment, net, was $41.8 million as of Sept. 30, 2026, consisting primarily of the Company’s commercial aircraft engines, and loans receivable and accrued interest totaled $20.9 million. The Company held neither asset at year-end 2025. Total stockholders’ equity was $136.4 million as of Sept. 30, 2026, compared with $239.4 million at Dec. 31, 2025.

 

Guidance

 

The Company now expects full-year 2026 revenue of $8 million to $9 million, compared with its previous guidance of $18 million to $22 million. The revised guidance reflects the Company’s decision to slow the pace of deployment of its initial AI compute capacity in response to rapidly rising compute pricing, equipment costs and financing costs, which accelerated following the midterm elections. The Company’s previous guidance assumed that initial AI compute capacity would be deployed and generating revenue in the fourth quarter of 2026. The Company no longer expects to recognize AI compute revenue in 2026, and fourth quarter revenue is expected to be generated primarily by its commercial aircraft engine portfolio.

 

The Company is maintaining its full-year 2027 revenue guidance of $125 million to $175 million. The Company is in discussions with multiple potential partners on how best to structure agreements in the current market environment. These structures could involve increases in GPU pricing, higher upfront customer deposits, changes in offtake duration and revisions to or terminations of existing contracts, each with varying trade-offs among revenue, EBITDA margins and cash flow. GPU pricing has risen, and the market is trending toward higher upfront deposits as a percentage of contract value for a variety of reasons, including the cost of GPU equipment and the cost of debt capital. The Company will update its guidance once its Dallas and North Carolina capacity is fully contracted and the mix of customer deposits and financing is finalized.

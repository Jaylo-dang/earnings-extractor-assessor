Yarrow Bioscience Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

Enrollment ongoing in the Phase 2a/2b trial evaluating YB-101, a potential first-in-class anti-TSHR antibody, in patients with Graves’ disease (with or without concomitant thyroid eye disease); Phase 2a topline results expected in the second half of 2027

Completed approximately $172.5 million underwritten public offering, further strengthening the Company’s financial position to advance clinical development of YB-101

Cash and cash equivalents of $268.9 million as of September 30, 2026; expected to fund operations into 2030

New Haven, CT—November 12, 2026—Yarrow Bioscience, Inc. (“Yarrow” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: YARW), a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing transformative therapies for autoimmune thyroid diseases, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.
“We made significant progress in the third quarter, including dosing our first patient in the Phase 2a portion of the DIRECTION-GD trial and strengthening our balance sheet through the completion of our merger and subsequent public offering,” said Rebecca V. Frey, PharmD, President and Chief Executive Officer of Yarrow. “The financing extends our expected cash runway into 2030 and positions us to advance YB-101 in Graves’ disease and prepare for our next steps in thyroid eye disease. We remain focused on executing the DIRECTION-GD trial as we look ahead to topline results from the Phase 2a portion of the trial and data from GenSci’s Phase 1 multiple ascending dose trial in TED, both of which we anticipate in the second half of 2027.”
Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights and Upcoming Milestones
YB-101: potential first-in-class anti-thyroid stimulating hormone receptor (“TSHR”) antibody for Graves’ disease (“GD”) and thyroid eye disease (“TED”)
•Enrollment is ongoing in the Phase 2a portion of the DIRECTION-GD trial, a Phase 2a/2b trial of YB-101 in patients with GD, with or without concomitant TED.
•Topline results from the Phase 2a portion of the trial are expected in the second half of 2027.
•The Phase 2b portion of the trial is expected to begin enrolling patients in the first half of 2028.

•Licensing partner, Changchun GeneScience Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. (“GenSci”), continues to advance YB-101 (also known as GenSci098) in Greater China. Data from GenSci’s ongoing Phase 1 multiple ascending dose (“MAD”) trial in TED are anticipated in the second half of 2027 and are expected to inform the Company’s global TED development plans.
Corporate Updates
•In July 2026, pre-merger Yarrow completed the merger with VYNE Therapeutics Inc. (the “Merger”). Following the completion of the Merger, the combined company began trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the ticker symbol “YARW.”
•In September 2026, the Company completed an underwritten public offering of 6,634,615 shares of common stock, including the full exercise of the underwriters’ option to purchase additional shares (the “September 2026 Offering”). The aggregate gross proceeds from the September 2026 Offering were approximately $172.5 million, before deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and other offering expenses.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Cash Position: As of September 30, 2026, Yarrow had cash and cash equivalents of $268.9 million, including the net proceeds from the September 2026 Offering. The Company expects its cash and cash equivalents to fund operations into 2030, including through the completion of the DIRECTION-GD trial and preparatory work for the Company’s next planned TED study.
•Research & Development Expenses: R&D expenses totaled $5.3 million for the third quarter of 2026.
•General & Administrative Expenses: G&A expenses totaled $5.5 million for the third quarter of 2026.
•Net Loss: Net loss totaled $9.7 million for the third quarter of 2026.
•Shares Outstanding: As of September 30, 2026, Yarrow had approximately 11.5 million shares of common stock issued and outstanding and pre-funded warrants to purchase approximately 23.7 million additional shares of common stock.
About YB-101
YB-101 is an investigational, potential first-in-class anti-TSHR monoclonal antibody designed to directly and rapidly disrupt the central mechanism of both GD and TED. YB-101 is currently being evaluated in the DIRECTION-GD trial, an ongoing Phase 2a/2b clinical trial in patients with GD, with or without concomitant TED. YB-101 has received Fast Track Designation from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (the “FDA”). Fast Track Designation is intended to facilitate the development and expedite the review of new therapies for serious conditions with unmet medical needs.

About the DIRECTION-GD Clinical Trial
DIRECTION-GD is a Phase 2a/2b randomized, blinded, placebo-controlled two-part trial evaluating YB-101 in patients with GD, with or without concomitant TED (NCT07682896). The Phase 2a portion (Part 1) is a proof-of-concept trial of YB-101 versus placebo aiming to enroll 32 patients across four cohorts. The trial will evaluate safety, pharmacokinetics (“PK”), pharmacodynamics (“PD”) and efficacy endpoints through 24 weeks, including the percentage of patients who are euthyroid and off anti-thyroid drugs, as well as relevant measures of orbitopathy in patients with concomitant TED. Topline data from the Phase 2a portion are expected in the second half of 2027.
The Phase 2b portion (Part 2) is expected to be conducted as a dose-finding trial and enroll approximately 200 patients. The selection of doses and dosing intervals for the Phase 2b portion is expected to be informed by the safety, efficacy, PK and PD data generated in the Phase 2a portion. The Phase 2b portion is anticipated to commence in the first half of 2028.

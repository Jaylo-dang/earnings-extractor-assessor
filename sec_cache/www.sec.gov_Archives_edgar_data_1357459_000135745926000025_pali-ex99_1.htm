Palisade Bio Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Advances PALI-2108 Phase 2 Clinical Development Programs

 

First Patient Dosed in Global Phase 2b ASCENTRA-UC Trial; Topline Results Expected in Q4 2027

FDA Clearance Received for Phase 2a ASCENTRA-CD Trial; Initiation of Enrollment Targeted for Q1 2027

New Clinical and Translational Data Presented at ACG and UEG Week 2026 Highlight PALI-2108's Targeted Profile
 

$113.6 Million in Cash and Cash Equivalents as of September 30, 2026

 

Denver, CO, November 10, 2026 — Palisade Bio, Inc. (Nasdaq: PALI) (“Palisade” or the “Company”), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing oral therapies for immune-mediated and fibrotic diseases, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update highlighting continued execution of its Phase 2 clinical development strategy for PALI-2108 across inflammatory bowel disease (“IBD”).

“ASCENTRA-UC is now underway, and our focus is on driving enrollment and executing the study to deliver topline results in the fourth quarter of 2027. With nearly all planned U.S. sites activated, we are expanding our global clinical footprint to build on that progress,” said JD Finley, Chief Executive Officer of Palisade Bio. “In parallel, we are preparing to initiate ASCENTRA-CD, with initiation of enrollment targeted for the first quarter of 2027. Our strong financial position supports execution across both programs as we work to establish PALI-2108’s potential as a differentiated, once-daily oral treatment for patients living with IBD.”

Recent Highlights

Initiated Global Phase 2b ASCENTRA-UC Clinical Trial

 

•
Dosed the first patient in ASCENTRA-UC on October 27, 2026, marking an important milestone in the clinical development of PALI-2108 for ulcerative colitis (“UC”).
•
Activated 29 of 30 planned U.S. clinical sites, supporting continued enrollment in the study.
•
Advanced global expansion toward approximately 115 total sites, with Health Canada CTA clearance received in September 2026, regulatory clearance received in the Republic of Georgia in October 2026 and additional regulatory submissions under review in Europe and other countries.
•
ASCENTRA-UC is expected to enroll approximately 204 patients with moderately to severely active UC and evaluate two once-daily oral doses of PALI-2108, 15 mg and 30 mg, compared with placebo.

•
The primary endpoint is clinical remission at Week 12, as measured by the Modified Mayo Score, with secondary endpoints including clinical response, endoscopic improvement and histologic-endoscopic mucosal improvement (“HEMI”).
•
Following the 12-week induction period, eligible patients will continue into a 36-week maintenance phase designed to evaluate the durability of remission and response through Week 48.
•
Topline results from the 12-week induction period are expected in Q4 2027.

 

Advanced Phase 2a ASCENTRA-CD Program in Crohn's Disease

 

•
The FDA cleared the IND application for PALI-2108 in Crohn's disease (“CD”) on September 22, 2026, enabling initiation of ASCENTRA-CD.
•
ASCENTRA-CD is a multicenter, open-label, single-arm Phase 2a study expected to enroll approximately 60 adults with moderate-to-severe CD.
•
The study will evaluate PALI-2108 at a target dose of 30 mg orally once daily.
•
The primary endpoint is endoscopic response at Week 12, as defined as a ≥ 50% reduction from baseline in the Simple Endoscopic Score for Crohn’s Disease (“SES-CD”), based on blinded central endoscopic review.
•
Initiation of enrollment is targeted for Q1 2027, with the primary efficacy readout expected in Q1 2028.
 

Presented New Clinical and Translational Data at ACG and UEG Week 2026

 

•
Presented five posters at ACG 2026, including two selected for Presidential Poster Awards, highlighting PALI-2108's clinical, pharmacokinetic, pharmacodynamic and mechanistic profile in inflammatory bowel disease.
•
Presented additional clinical and translational findings in one oral presentation and three moderated posters at UEG Week 2026, including endoscopic improvement within two weeks in an exploratory, open-label Phase 1b cohort of five patients with fibrostenotic Crohn's disease and further evidence of targeted intestinal drug exposure.
•
Collectively, these findings support the continued phase 2 development of PALI-2108 in UC and CD.

 

“Now that ASCENTRA-UC is underway, our priority is to maintain enrollment momentum and deliver the clinical data needed to establish PALI-2108’s potential in ulcerative colitis,” said Mitch Jones, M.D., Ph.D., President and Chief Medical Officer of Palisade Bio. “We expect the results to provide a clearer understanding of its ability to induce remission, reduce intestinal inflammation and support sustained disease control. In Crohn’s disease, we are focused on preparing ASCENTRA-CD for initiation of enrollment in the first quarter of 2027. We are pursuing these programs with the goal of developing a treatment that, if approved, could offer patients an effective, well-tolerated treatment that fits into daily life through once-daily oral dosing.”

Summary of Financial Results

Research and development expenses were $10.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase primarily reflects expenses associated with advancing PALI-2108 into Phase 2 clinical development, including clinical trial start-up activities, manufacturing and clinical supply, and supporting preclinical and translational research, as well as higher employee-related expenses, including increased non-cash stock-based compensation expense.

General and administrative expenses were $5.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The year-over-year increase reflects the Company’s expanded operating activities and higher employee-related expenses, including increased non-cash stock-based compensation expense.

 

Net loss was $14.6 million, or $0.07 per share, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $2.9 million, or $0.38 per share, for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $113.6 million in cash and cash equivalents, which the Company believes will fund operations through key Phase 2 clinical milestones, including the anticipated primary efficacy readouts from ASCENTRA-UC in the fourth quarter of 2027 and ASCENTRA-CD in the first quarter of 2028.

 

About PALI-2108

 

PALI-2108 is an investigational, once-daily oral PDE4 inhibitor prodrug designed for localized bioactivation in the terminal ileum and colon. Its active metabolite, PALI-0008, potently inhibits PDE4 isoforms A, B and D, increasing intracellular cAMP to modulate inflammatory signaling. Phase 1 studies have provided evidence of high intestinal exposure, measurable systemic exposure supportive of once-daily dosing, favorable short-term tolerability and early clinical activity, supporting Phase 2 development in UC and Crohn’s disease. PALI-2108 has not been approved for any indication.

 

About Palisade Bio

 

Palisade Bio is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing oral therapies for immune-mediated and fibrotic diseases. Its lead program, PALI-2108, is in Phase 2 development in inflammatory bowel disease. The Company is actively enrolling patients in its Phase 2b ASCENTRA-UC study, while advancing its Phase 2a ASCENTRA-CD study toward commencement.

For more information, please go to www.palisadebio.com.

Beachbody (BODi) Reports Third Quarter Financial Results

 

Net Income and Operating Income Reported for Fifth Consecutive Quarter

Net Income and Adjusted EBITDA Exceed High End of Guidance

Revenue Within Guidance

Twelfth Consecutive Quarter of Positive Adjusted EBITDA

 

 

 

El Segundo, Calif. (November 10, 2026) – The Beachbody Company, Inc. (NASDAQ: BODi) (“BODi” or the “Company”), the proactive wellness company delivering nutrition, supplements, and proven fitness programs that help people take control of their health inside and out, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Net income and adjusted EBITDA exceeded the high end of our guidance and revenue met expectations as we continue to transform our business model,” said Carl Daikeler, co-founder and BODi’s Chief Executive Officer. “As the proactive wellness company delivering nutrition, supplements and proven fitness programs that help people take control of their health, we are executing on our omni-channel strategy to place our iconic brands wherever shoppers are. Our innovation pipeline is strong and we expect to continue to benefit from our compelling digital fitness content along with our premium nutritional products as consumers increasingly focus on complete wellness solutions.”

 

"Our third quarter results mark our twelfth consecutive quarter of positive Adjusted EBITDA and our fifth consecutive quarter of double-digit Adjusted EBITDA margins, reflecting the operating discipline we have built into this company,” said Mark Goldston, BODi’s Executive Chairman. “Additional cost efficiencies are lowering our breakeven point even further and our multi-channel distribution strategy is gaining traction as we bring on new partners and expand our rollout across retail, DTC and Amazon. Our focus on the wellness category, under the BODi Wellness umbrella, significantly broadens our total addressable market and we are well positioned to capitalize on this massive opportunity.”

About BODi and The Beachbody Company, Inc.

 

BODi is the proactive wellness company delivering nutrition, supplements, and proven fitness programs that help people take control of their health inside and out. With nearly three decades of experience, BODi, formerly Beachbody, has evolved from a leader in home fitness into a comprehensive health and fitness ecosystem designed to help people achieve their goals and lead healthier, more fulfilling lives. Anchored by science-backed nutrition solutions like Shakeology and supported by its portfolio of proven fitness and habit-building programs, including P90X and INSANITY, BODi is creating a more accessible and effective path to long-term health. Since its inception, BODi has supported more than 30 million customers in achieving lasting results. The company continues to innovate across nutrition and digital fitness to deliver simple, proven solutions for modern lifestyles. For more information, please visit TheBeachBodyCompany.com.

Adjusted EBITDA

We use Adjusted EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP performance measure, to supplement our results presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America ("GAAP").
